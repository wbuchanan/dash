```
markers: scatter, connected, dot, mcolor(navy) all opaque #1A476F; mcolor(navy%50) fill-opacity:0.50
```

The direction of the blend is the scheme's `system naturally_white`, not its
background. `#include s2color` + `color background black` still blends towards
white (#8DA3B7). `#include s1rcolor` + `color background white` still darkens
(#0D2438). `#include s2color` + `system naturally_white 0` darkens: default bar
#153959, fintensity(50) #0D2438. Rule: with naturally_white 1, f<=1 gives
255-(255-c)f and f>1 gives c/f. With naturally_white 0, the result is c*f,
clamped to 0..255, and f<0 is read as 0. Rounding is half up.

## 6. Decisions for the maintainer

1. **Vertical y tick labels on every default dashboard.** In 11 schemes, including the default s2color, twoway draws y labels vertically. The options are (a) match, with `ylabel(, angle(0))` as the escape; (b) keep horizontal as a documented difference. Recommendation: (a), consistent with M1 "MATCH twoway".
2. **Opaque markers.** Alpha 0.8 helps overplotting in a browser, but twoway draws markers opaque. The options are (a) match; (b) keep 0.8 and document it. Recommendation: (a). `malpha()` and %NN remain.
3. **Every existing dashboard changes look**, as in P1.12. Recommendation: accept it and record it in CHANGELOG as a behaviour change.
4. **Split.** Recommendation: two plan sections. P1.2a "chrome" covers A-D and F (title/subtitle/note/caption, axis titles, ticks, angle, grid, economist axes). P1.2b "intensity" covers E (markers, fills, fintensity, ci band). Batch P1.2b with P1.4 and P1.13, which share `_blend_intensity` and `_scheme_alpha`.

   `map_scatter` twice (:13356, :13361), so its markers draw at 0.8 in both
   modes (probe_c.py: standalone and served `rconnected [('Scatter', 0.8)]`).
   Add it to approach E and to TestMarkersAreOpaque. rscatter and scatteri
   are 1.0.
2. **fintensity rule above 100.** "f>1 gives c/f" holds only up to
   fintensity(5000): navy gives #010102 = c/50 (`spot3.do`). Navy at 6000,
   7500, 9000, 9999, 10000, 20000 and 100000 all give #010304, where c/f would
   give #000102..#000000 (`spot3.do`, `spot4.do`, `spot2.do`). White follows
   c/f to 9999 (#030303). twoway accepts all of these with rc 0, and
   dash.sthlp:691 says fintensity() takes "any number". So the parser must
   clamp, never refuse. The proposed 10000 bound gives #000101 where twoway
   gives #010304; see decision 5. s1rcolor fi(50) gives #808000 and fi(10000)
   gives #FFFF00 (c*f clamped), which confirms the naturally_white 0 rule.
3. **`_dash_textbox_place` 2-4 and 8-10 → right and left Titles is wrong.**
   BokehJS `TitleView.angle` is `panel.get_label_angle_heuristic("parallel")`
   (bokeh 3.9 bokeh.js:27351ff), so a Title on a left or right panel is drawn
   vertical. twoway draws `position(3)` text horizontal. Scheme defaults use
   only 11, 12, 1 and 7, so the defaults are unaffected. P2.4's reuse must use
   a Label, or an above/below Title, for those clocks.
4. **Economist's note.** twoway draws it in the title's band: box y 55-140,
   NTE baseline 121, TTL baseline 157 (`tw_sc_econ.svg`). As a separate
   above-panel Title it gets its own row. Accept and document this, or place
   it beside the title (decision 6).
5. **ROADMAP.md:2051 nuance.** Under s1rcolor, one bar at alpha 0.8 over the
   #000000 plot region composites to #CCCC00, which is twoway's colour. The
   defect shows where glyphs overlap. `twoway bar mpg rep78` draws 69
   overlapping bars at 5 x values, uniformly #CCCC00, where dash compounds
   them towards #FFFF00. The cause is the wrong key, `intensity bar`.
   twoway area under s1rcolor is #CCCC00 (`tw_area_s1r.svg`), while dash draws
   #ffff00 at 1.0 because dash-s1rcolor.scheme:85-92 has `intensity area full`.
6. **twoway ignores `intensity bar` and `intensity area`; measured.** A user
   scheme with `#include s2color` + `intensity bar inten40` + `intensity area
   inten40` leaves bar and area at #486C8C. `intensity p1bar inten40` +
   `intensity p1area inten40` gives #A3B5C5 for both (`spot5.do`). This
   confirms the p#bar and p#area chains in A and E.
7. **Help.** dash.sthlp:2583-2584 is the CI-band sub-option table, not a
