## 3. Proposed approach (two plan sections)

### P1.21a: positions and sub-option grammar (both modes, before and after a move)

1. **dash.ado and Mata: refuse what twoway refuses, in its order.** Extend the P0.5 raw-text check: `_dash_axreq_add` already records every line copy as typed (family 7). For each line row, dash.ado pre-computes a line-argument verdict with Stata's own `numlist` (no max(), so twoway's 2,500 cap applies). A copy holding a backtick, `$` or `'` is never put on numlist's line and is invalid, as `_dash_axis_expand` treats such runs. `_dash_axreq_check` then tests each line row, after its axis checks, in twoway's order:
   - a sub-option word that is not in the table (lower case only, at least the minimum length), a parenthesis mismatch, a flag or sub-option given twice in one copy, or extra text after the comma: `option <w> not allowed` / `option <n>() not allowed`, r(198);
   - empty positions with sub-options: `something required`, r(100);
   - a numlist failure: `invalid line argument, <text>`, r(198).

   All messages go through `_dash_say`. This is decision D1/D2 (§5).
2. **dash.ado: expand.** A new `_dash_line_expand` (modelled on `_dash_axis_expand`, never refusing) runs on the merged `g_xline`, `g_yline`, `g_xline2` and `g_yline2` before the spec is written. It splits at top-level commas; a segment starting with a digit, sign or `.` is a copy's positions and is replaced by `r(numlist)`, uniq-ed. The spec then holds plain numbers: `2000 2500 3000, lcolor(red), 4000`.
3. **dash_render.py fallback, for a spec not written by dash.ado.** In `_dash_refline_instances`:
   - expand position runs with `_dash_expand_numlist(tokens, limit=2500)` (called, not edited; it is CRITICAL);
   - dedupe in order;
   - recognise the honoured set through the shared abbreviation table (`_dash_subopt_canon` plus a refline table `st/noex/ex/lst/lc/lw/lp/la/ax`, built in P1.19);
   - record extend/noextend/style() for P1.21b, and leave the rest named.
4. **`lstyle()`.** In `_dash_refline_draw`, the base style becomes the named linestyle when given, else the scheme's xyline. lcolor/lwidth/lpattern still override it in any order, `none` draws nothing, and an unknown name gives twoway's wording ("named style X not found; default attributes used") with foreground's attributes. Resolve through P1.14's `SchemeParser.style_bundle("line", name)`, extended past p1..p15 to the named linestyles (foreground, grid, xyline, refline, ci, ci2, p#line, none and the other `linestyle-*.style` chains) with the keys `scripts/extract_scheme_truth.py` resolves from Stata's own style files into the 20 dash-*.scheme files (D5).
5. **`lalign()`.** Accept inside/outside/center in silence, since twoway draws no difference. An unknown name gets twoway's note text ("class alignstroke").
6. **Cost and safety.** Collect each figure's Spans and assign `fig.center` once, instead of one `add_layout` per value. Measured (`span_cost*.py`, bokeh 3.9): one-at-a-time adds of 2,500 Spans take 5.54 s and 10,000 take 69.96 s; one bulk assignment takes 1.32 s for 2,500 and 5.53 s for 10,000 (0.98 MB). Add a per-graph ceiling (D3, recommended 10,000) with a note beyond it, in both modes. Each copy's 2,500 cap is already twoway's.
7. **Served app.** Add every new constant or helper to the copy lists in `ServerAppBuilder._glyph_layer_lines` (dash_render.py:32834; lists at :32931-32944). `test_resource_limits::test_no_function_in_the_app_reads_an_undefined_global` guards this.
8. **Docs.**
   - dash.sthlp:2755-2756: change the synopsis to `xline(linearg)` (numlist [, sub-options]).
   - :3349-3354: rewrite the Reference lines paragraph (lstyle, lalign, the extent).
   - :618: the sub-option sentence.
   - Also: a CHANGELOG "Results that change" line, BUGS.md entries per rule 7, GAPS2 B22 "Tracked", TWOWAY_COVERAGE_AUDIT :139, and the DROPIN tracking line.

### P1.21b: extend, noextend and style() (after P1.3)

twoway's unextended line spans exactly the axis's scale (its inner hull), and the extended line spans the plot region. In dash:
- An extended copy stays a `Span`.
- An unextended copy, or an end the scheme's `yesno xyline_extend_low/high` says not to extend, is drawn as a Bokeh `Arrow(end=None)` in data units from the hull's low end to its high end. An extended end runs to the range end.

Feasibility was rendered in headless Chrome (scratch `arrow_demo.png`, `node_demo.png`):
- An `Arrow` is one path, so dashes draw correctly, and it is clipped by the frame.
- A degenerate `BoxAnnotation` with frame `Node` insets draws a horizontal dashed line as solid, because two strokes overlap. It is unusable for stcolor's dashed xyline.

The hull must be the one P1.3 gives every axis (`_dash_hull_ranges` and `_dash_range_hull`). The plan:
- `_dash_reference_lines` records the pending unextended copies on the figure.
- `_dash_hull_ranges` (LOW risk; it runs for single figures and by() panels) resolves them.
- P1.3's planned `_dash_refinish_axes` (served) and `_DASH_AXIS_FINISH_JS` (saved page) re-set the Arrow ends after every filter move, so both modes agree after a move.
- `style(default|extended|unextended)` maps to the two flags. `extend` beats `noextend` and `style(unextended)`. An unknown style is noted as twoway does ("class gridlinestyle").
- SchemeParser reads the `yesno xyline_extend_*` keys (default yes), transcribed into the 20 scheme files.
- `test_mode_parity._annotations` must profile Arrow annotations, or the parity guard is blind to them.

## 6. Decisions for the maintainer

- **D1. Refuse vs note (DROPIN §4; P1.19 D2).**
  - Options: (a) refuse r(198)/r(100) in twoway's words and order, as P0.5 does for axis(#); (b) keep rc 0 and a note.
  - Implications: (a) changes existing do-files that pass `xline(abc)` or `xline(1(0)5)`, and flips SR-06 and the parity notes. (b) keeps dash drawing what twoway refuses.
  - Recommendation: (a).
- **D2. Capitals (P1.19 D1).** `NOEXTEND` and `LSTYLE()` are refused by twoway. Recommendation: refuse as twoway does, decided once with P1.19.
- **D3. Per-graph ceiling on reference lines.**
  - twoway draws any number of copies of up to 2,500 each. dash, one at a time, needs 70 s for 10,000.
  - Options: (a) a ceiling with a note (recommended: 10,000 per graph, a `$dash_max_*`-style limit in the resource envelope); (b) no ceiling, bulk add only.
  - Recommendation: (a), since security is paramount.
- **D4. How noextend is drawn.**
  - Options: (a) exact, the hull from P1.3, as an `Arrow`, re-finished after moves (recommended; schedule P1.21b after P1.3); (b) approximate now with a frame-inset BoxAnnotation (dashed horizontal lines render solid, as measured); (c) keep it named and document it.
- **D5. lstyle scope.**
  - Options: (a) every Stata linestyle name, resolved from the style-file chain into the scheme files (exact, data-driven, shared with P1.14); (b) p1..p15, foreground, grid, xyline, refline, ci and none only, and name the rest.
  - Recommendation: (a). The help's own examples are foreground and grid.

No decision is needed for lalign (twoway draws no difference) or for deduping (twoway uses `list uniq`).

## 7. Effort, risk, split, feasibility

- Effort: P1.21a M, P1.21b M, L in total. DROPIN's "S" underestimates it: the refusals, the lstyle resolver and the extent were not counted.
- Risk: yellow. The refusals change accepted input, several tests flip, the scheme files are re-transcribed, and the HIGH-risk served copy list is touched.
- Split: yes, into P1.21a (positions, grammar, refusals, lstyle, lalign, cost cap; with P1.19) and P1.21b (extend, noextend, style() and the scheme extend keys; after P1.3).
- Feasibility here: full. StataNow 19.5 gives every truth through SVG and class members, and headless Chrome (`responsive_probe.find_chrome()`) checks the saved page after a move.
- Residual to file (P2): twoway draws an out-of-range xline outside the plot region (x=186 over the y labels, unclipped in its SVG), where dash's line is clipped by the frame.

1. **A numlist copy re-styles the copy before it (silent wrong colour, dash.ado path).**
   - `yline(20, lcolor(red)) yline(10(5)40, lcolor(blue))`: dash draws one Span at 20 in #0000ff (c2.html). twoway draws red at 20 plus 7 blue lines (t1b.svg).
   - Cause: `_dash_refline_instances` starts a new copy only at a `float()` token. `10(5)40` goes to *bad*, and the next segment's `lcolor(blue)` overwrites copy 1's red. Unit check: `_dash_refline_instances("20, lcolor(red), 10(5)40, lcolor(blue)")` returns `[([20.0], {'lcolor': 'blue'})]`.
