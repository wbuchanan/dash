# Section 44-01: Axis titles: orientation, right-axis reading, two lines, the ... rule

> Part of: WP-44 (Axes, audit package 9) · Items: AX-13, PIC-12, MS-13, MS-11 · Risk: yellow · Batch: 44.1 · Depends on: none (WP-43 landed) · Blocks: 44-02 · Tasks: 9, 4 commits · Case-id prefix: Q4401

## Overview

**What is wrong today.** Four ways an axis title differs from twoway's:

- **AX-13 (ST-13).** "Axis title textbox sub-options other than size/color are named: orientation() (Bokeh axis_label_orientation never set), justification(), placement(), box, margin() ..." (audit :440). `_DASH_TEXTBOX_HONOURED` (dash_render.py:53826-53827) is `{size, color, prefix, suffix, axis}`; `_dash_textbox` (:53842) names everything else on `xtitle()`/`ytitle()`, and `_dash_style_axis_titles` (:53961-53995) sets only the font size, colour and alpha. `ytitle("Y", orientation(horizontal))` -- a common idiom -- prints "ytitle() sub-option(s) not implemented and ignored: orientation(horizontal)" and draws the title turned.
- **PIC-12.** "A right-hand y title reads top to bottom; twoway's reads upward (every economist graph, any yaxis(2))" (audit :485). twoway writes every y title `rotate(-90)` (appendix :3118). `axis_label_orientation` is set nowhere in dash_render.py; its only occurrence is the copy list `_DASH_ALT_TITLE_PROPS` (:45398-45402), so Bokeh's default `"parallel"` applies, which BokehJS turns +90 degrees on a right panel (`_angle_lookup.right.parallel = pi2`, bokeh.js 3.9 :27673-27698).
- **MS-13.** "ytitle(A) ytitle(B, suffix) is joined on one line; twoway draws two lines for axis titles" (audit :482). `_dash_rep_merge` (dash_parse.mata:11140) joins category-T copies with one blank (`if (suf & has2) body = strtrim(b1) + " " + strtrim(b2)`, :11170). twoway draws one line `A B` for `title()`/`note()` copies (E09, B11, which dash matches) but two lines for an axis title (E08, B17).
- **MS-11 (RH-17).** "twoway's '<first title>...' x title (different x across y-axis groups, or upright plus turned layers) is not drawn" (audit :479). `_dash_axis_default_titles` (dash_render.py:39809-39841) applies the first-equals-last rule over every layer on the axis; twoway applies it per plot region and then joins regions with "..." (twowaygraph_g.class `create_axes`, :822-851). `(scatter mpg weight) (scatter price length, yaxis(2))` is titled `Weight (lbs.)...` by twoway and untitled by dash.

**After this section**, in both modes: every axis title draws `orientation()`, `justification()`, `alignment()`, `placement()`, `margin()`, `bmargin()`, `linegap()` and `tstyle()`, and the box family (`box`, `bcolor()`, `fcolor()`, `lcolor()`, `lwidth()`, `lpattern()`, `bexpand`, `width()`, `height()`) -- through a tagged `Title` composite on the left, bottom and top, and through WP-43's overlay route on the right (A-5); every right-hand y title reads upward, markup runs included; axis-title `suffix`/`prefix` copies stack on separate lines; and the default titles follow twoway's region rule with its `...`. Nothing drawn prints a note, and no residual is left.

**Why one section.** All four are the text of an axis title: the sub-option reader (`_dash_textbox`, `_DASH_TEXTBOX_HONOURED`), the late styling of every Axis model, the Mata merge of repeated title options and the default-title rule. The index makes 44-01 batch 44.1's owner of `_dash_style_axis_titles`, `_dash_axis_default_titles` and the axis-title rows of `_dash_rep_merge`.

**Test order and commit rule.** Four commits in the manifest's order: `(WP-44 AX-13)`, `(WP-44 PIC-12)`, `(WP-44 MS-13)`, `(WP-44 MS-11)`. 44-01-01 writes the extract script (all four items' probes), runs it, and writes ONLY AX-13's regression tests and its truth block, runs them on the unmodified tree, saves `<scratch>/44-01-01-failure.txt`, and does not commit. 44-01-02 makes AX-13 green and commits it with the fixture. ## Risk: [yellow] -- every axis title of every figure passes the new late helper, and the title merge rule is shared by every title option

**Why.** `_dash_axis_title_look` runs on every figure in both modes; the Mata merge category changes how repeated axis titles reach the spec; the default-title rule is used by every graph without an explicit title.

**Risk factors.**
