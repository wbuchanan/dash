# Section 41-04: by(): at() holes, legloc/plegend, coloruseplegend keys, hole legend scale

> Part of: WP-41 (by() fidelity, P6) · Items: BY-3, BY-13, BY-14, PIC-13 · Risk: yellow · Batch: 41.3 · Depends on: 41-02 · Blocks: 41-05 · Tasks: 8, 4 commits · Case-id prefix: Q4104

## Overview

**What is wrong today.**
- **BY-3.** "An at() hole for a legend or colour key that is not drawn is not reserved in the grid." twoway's SetLegend/SetClegend "set legend_hole/clegend_hole from at() whether or not the key is drawn (bygraph_g.class:612-618, :670-676)": `by(foreign, legend(at(1)))` with one plot, `(scatter)(lfit) by(foreign, legend(off at(1)))` and `colorvar(headroom) by(foreign, clegend(at(1)))` without `on` "all give a 2x2 grid with cell 1 empty"; "full_n, and so iscale, includes the hole"; with no colorvar `clegend(at(1))` makes no hole. dash puts both panels in one row: `out["legend_at"]` is set only inside `if drawn:` (dash_render.py:55794-55874) and `clegend_at` only under `words & {"on", "draw"}` (:55878-56024). Also `_dash_share_by_scales` computes the margin multiplier's `full_n` without the holes (:47961-47966) though its comment says it counts them.
- **BY-13.** "by(, legloc() clegloc() plegend() plegloc()) are named and ignored": twoway's `legloc(pos(6))` draws "exactly as by(foreign, legend(pos(6)))", `clegend(on) clegloc(pos(6))` moves the key below the panels. `_dash_parse_by` lets only `legend(...)`/`clegend(...)` through (:36373-36374); `_dash_by_axes_options` collects only their copies (:55234-55238).
- **BY-14.** "coloruseplegend under by(): plegend(on) draws nothing, and clegend(on) draws a discrete Legend with '(missing)' where twoway draws a colour-bar key": twoway T11 draws "a plot legend at the right", T12 "a colour-bar key labelled 1-5 with the upright title 'Repair record 1978'"; dash's clegend(on) clones the coloruseplegend Legend (`_dash_by_legend_cell(scheme, "dash_by_clegend", ..., list(key.items), "c")`, :55938-55941) with its "(missing)" item (:16600-16607); no by()-level plegend path exists.
- **PIC-13.** "A legend in a by() at() hole keeps full-size text; twoway scales it (0.85x)": gr_example #65 `by(region, legend(at(4) pos(0)))` key labels 2.953 rs against #64's 3.474; dash "Labels stay 10pt (3.474 rs) with the full 31x13 px key box". `_dash_by_legend_cell` (:55644-55681) copies panel 1's sizes; `_dash_by_scale_panel` leaves legends alone (:36860).

**After this section.** An at() hole is reserved whenever at() is given and its key object exists (a legend always; a colour key when the graph draws one), counted in full_n for the layout, the size multiplier and the margin; legloc()/clegloc()/plegloc() merge into legend()/clegend()/plegend() placement; by(, plegend()) draws coloruseplegend's keys as an overall plot legend, and by(, clegend(on)) under coloruseplegend draws a stepped colour-bar key without "(missing)"; a legend in a hole is drawn at by()'s size multiplier.

**Why together.** All four are `_dash_by_keys` and its consumers `_dash_by_side`/`_dash_by_side_grid`: one function's three branches and the hole bookkeeping.

**Test order and commit rule.** 41-04-01 measures all four into one fixture (no commit). Then per item in the manifest's order (BY-3, BY-13, BY-14, PIC-13) a test task (failure saved, no commit) and a fix task committing `(WP-41 <id>)`, except PIC-13 whose single task writes, saves, fixes and commits. 

### Section decisions

- **D-41-04.a -- the hole rule.** `_dash_by_hole_ats(go, has_colour_key)` (new) reads at() from by()'s merged legend()/legloc() and clegend()/clegloc() copies: the legend hole is `at` whenever given (every twoway graph has a legend object); the colour-key hole is `at` when given and the graph has a colour key (a colorvar() layer or a contour). `_dash_by_keys` reports these as `legend_at`/`clegend_at` drawn or not; `_dash_share_by_scales` passes them to `_dash_by_positions` for its margin `full_n`. An undrawn key's cell is left empty (GridBox has no child there).
- **D-41-04.b -- loc words merge last.** `legloc(x)` is appended after the legend() copies (twoway passes `` `legend' `legloc' `` to SetLegend), `clegloc` after clegend(), `plegloc` after plegend(); the rightmost of each sub-option wins as for repeated copies today.
- **D-41-04.c -- plegend cell.** by(, plegend(on|draw)) under a coloruseplegend layer takes panel 1's coloruseplegend Legend (tagged `_dash_useplegend` in `_apply_colorvar`) into a `dash_by_plegend` cell through `_dash_by_legend_cell(scheme, "dash_by_plegend", side, key, items, "p", panels=figs)`; placed by its position() (default `zyx2_position`, measured right) like the legend cell. Its entries' order and texts are CV-4's (42-04) and stay as panel 1's key draws them today.
- **D-41-04.d -- clegend under coloruseplegend.** A `dash_by_clegend` cell holding a horizontal-or-upright `ColorBar` (orientation as the clegend side gives) over `LinearColorMapper(palette=<item colours, "(missing)"/otherlabel dropped>, low=0, high=n)` with `FixedTicker(ticks=[i + 0.5 ...])` and `major_label_overrides` = the item labels, titled with the Legend's title. The key's z-axis styling is CV-3's (42-03).
- **D-41-04.e -- hole key scale.** A legend drawn in a hole is scaled once by `_dash_by_size_mult(go.get("by"), full_n)` through `_dash_apply_scale(cell, m)` in `_dash_by_side`; a colour key in a hole is scaled the same way only if 41-04-01's K12 shows twoway scales it (ratio of its label size to the non-hole key's not 1 within 0.01).

## Risk: [yellow] -- by()'s key cells and grid holes in both modes

**Why.** Reserving holes changes the grid of every by() graph that names at() without drawing the key; the key branches are shared by both modes and by the relevel plan (:55995-56017).

**Risk factors.** 1. `_dash_by_side_grid` places hole cells only for kinds present (:56644-56647); an undrawn key must still shift the panels (via `_dash_by_positions`) but add no child. 2. `_dash_apply_scale` is idempotent per model (`_dash_scaled`): 41-03's by(, scale()) pass in `_dash_layout_cell` skips a cell already scaled here, so the hole legend's m must already include by()'s scale (it does: 41-03 folds it into `_dash_by_size_mult`). 3. The discrete clegend cell is re-bound to the relevel plan (:55995-56017); the colour-bar form must keep the relevel hook or say why not (relevel of a coloruseplegend key moves its colours, not its levels).

**Mitigation.** Fixture rows per case; grid-position unit tests (both modes); browser tests at 1000 px for K01, K08, K09, K11.
