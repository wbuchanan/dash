> Part of: WP-40 (Resource limits) · Items: plat:PT-8, plat:PT-9, plat:PT-10, stat:PT-15, sil:SD-1, AX-16, sil:SD-11, sil:SD-12, sil:SD-13 · Risk: green · Batch: 40.2 · Depends on: 40-01 · Blocks: none · Tasks: 4, 9 commits · Case-id prefix: Q4002

## Overview

**What is wrong today.** twoway has no caps; dash has many, and the help lists some under Resource limits (dash.sthlp:2933-3086) and none under "Differences from twoway" (audit package 5, :185-221; appendix :1143-1148, :1286-1291, :1620-1625, :1888-1893, :2286-2303, :2672-2677, :2720-2743, :2792-2797). plat:PT-8 (with syn:SD-16, sil:SD-22): r(134) above 5,000,000 embedded cells (ceiling 20,000,000, about 10 million observations of one y and one x), 100 plots, 10,000 reference lines and 1,000 added texts. Ruling P4-3 removed the embedded-cell and page-bytes caps in batch 33.1 (section 33-15) and keeps every count cap (graphs, plots, by groups, tabs, filters, reference lines, added texts, table rows, images), documented (Q44); so what plat:PT-8 leaves to this section is to say that dash, like twoway, has no size cap, and to list the count caps. plat:PT-9: the plot limit is per graph where twoway's is 100 variables per plot (limits.sthlp:104): dash takes `scatter y1-y100 x` (twoway r(103) "too many variables specified") and refuses `(scatter y1-y60 x) (scatter y61-y120 x)`, which twoway draws. plat:PT-10 (BY-22): by() draws the first 100 groups (ceiling 1,000) with a note; twoway draws every group. stat:PT-15 (DT-17's n()/bin() part): `n()` above 10,000 and `bin()` above 1,000 are refused r(198) in dash's words where twoway draws; `lpoly degree()` above 30 is fitted at a lower degree. sil:SD-1: histogram `width()` or `discrete` needing more than 1,000 bins, empty ones counted, is rebinned -- `width()` dropped, `discrete` squeezed into 1,000 bars -- where twoway draws only the occupied bins (`histogram price, discrete` 74 bars), and the help says discrete draws "one bar per distinct value" (:3990-3991, Appendix B HR-19). AX-16 (sil:SD-10): an axis option asking for more than 2,000 ticks keeps the default ticks with a note (twoway draws 3,501), `#N` above 2,000 is clamped silently. sil:SD-11: `msize()` above 1,000 units is drawn at the default with "not a recognised size" where the help says "any number" (:1176-1178, HR-19). sil:SD-12: fpfit/fpfitci above 20,000 distinct x are evaluated on a 20,000-point grid, silently. sil:SD-13: a `text()` whose globals expand past 4,000 characters is refused r(198) (documented at :784-785 and :5232-5234, not under Differences).

**What will be true after.** Ruling M10 / Q44 with P4-3 (and HR-21): the help says that dash, like twoway, has no limit on the size of the data or of the page, and every count cap is kept and documented in two places: a one-line sub-point under a new Differences item "Resource limits" linking to Resource limits, and the full statement, with twoway's answer, in Resource limits. Caps that have a global stay in its table; caps that are fixed get a new "Fixed limits" paragraph there, including 34-09's bound of 256 axis ranges that a build asks Stata's tick routine for (A-18: a count bound, not a size cap). No image is limited by its file size: only `$dash_max_images` bounds image panels (A-18). The two false sentences of HR-19 assigned here (msize "any number", discrete "one bar per distinct value") are corrected. No code changes (the msize note's wording is WP-49's, LT-25).
