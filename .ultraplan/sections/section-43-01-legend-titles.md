# Section 43-01: Legend title and subtitle styles; edge titles refused

> Part of: WP-43 (Legend, titles and added text) · Items: LT-1, LT-15, LT-13 · Risk: red · Batch: 43.1 · Depends on: none · Blocks: 43-02 · Tasks: 7, 3 commits · Case-id prefix: Q4301

## Overview

**What is wrong today.** LT-1 (audit :358-360, appendix legend-titles-text LT-1): "legend(title()/subtitle()) are drawn at key-text size and colour, not the scheme's leg_title/leg_subtitle styles". Measured by the audit: under stcolor twoway draws `legend(title("T"))` at 157.44 SVG px against 112.56 px keys, under s2color at 139.96 px in dknavy `#1E2D53` against 99.99 px black keys, and `legend(subtitle("S"))` at 123.69 px under stcolor; dash copies `label_text_font_size` and `label_text_color` into `title_text_*` for both (`_dash_legend_box`, dash_render.py:39260-39304) and never reads `textboxstyle leg_title`/`leg_subtitle` (no dash-*.scheme carries them). LT-15 (audit :391-393): "legend title()/subtitle() sub-options (position(), box ...) are dropped silently; subtitle size()/color() are ignored": twoway moves `legend(title("T", position(5)))` below the keys at the right, frames `title("T", box)`, and draws `subtitle("S", size(large) color(red))` large and red; dash reads only the title's `color()`/`size()` and prints nothing. LT-13 (audit :385-387): "legend(t1title() ... b2title()) is accepted and named where twoway refuses r(198)" -- twoway says `option t1title() not allowed` for t1, b1, l1, r2, t2 and b2 (measured), while stage 5 of both legend sub-option tables (dash_render.py:53561-53566, dash_parse.mata:9747-9754) lists the eight edge titles, so dash returns rc 0 with "legend() sub-option(s) not implemented and ignored".

**After this section** the legend's title and subtitle are drawn in the scheme's `leg_title` and `leg_subtitle` text boxes (heading and subheading under s2color and stcolor), with `size()`, `color()` and `tstyle()` overriding, in both modes; their `position()`, `justification()`, `box`, `nobox`, the box colours, `lwidth()`, `lpattern()` and `margin()` are drawn; a subtitle below a title is drawn in its own style as a composite (D-43-01.c); every other textbox sub-option of the legend's title or subtitle is named once; and `legend(t1title() ...)` through `legend(b2title())` are refused `option <name>() not allowed`, r(198), as twoway refuses them. The composite is placed by one layout-time dispatcher per figure that every later WP-43 composite joins (R4-7), is scaled by `scale()` (R4-4), and follows the legend into by()'s legend cell (R4-8).

**Why these share a section.** All three live in the legend's title block and its sub-option table, which this section owns (`_dash_apply_legend` title block = `_dash_legend_box`'s title and subtitle loops; `_DASH_LEGEND_SUBOPT_TABLE` and its Mata twin). The dispatcher, its scale rule and its by() move are built here because LT-15's subtitle is the first composite.

**Test order and commit rule (BRIEF).** Three cycles, three commits, in the manifest's order: LT-1 (43-01-01 red tests and fixture, saved failure, no commit; 43-01-02 fix; 43-01-03 certify, flip, document, commit `(WP-43 LT-1)`), LT-15 (43-01-04 red; 43-01-05 spike and fix; 43-01-06 commit `(WP-43 LT-15)`), LT-13 (43-01-07: red, fix, commit `(WP-43 LT-13)`).
