# Section 47-03: Outline, marker and dot alignment

> Part of: WP-47 (Plot-option fidelity, P15) · Items: basic:PT-4, basic:PT-6, basic:PT-5 · Risk: red · Batch: 47.1 · Depends on: none · Blocks: 47-06, 47-04 · Tasks: 7, 3 commits · Case-id prefix: Q4703

## Overview

**What is wrong today.** twoway strokes every closed shape's outline *inside* its edge by default (scheme `alignstroke ... inside`) and moves it with `lalign()`, `mlalign()` and `dlalign()`; Bokeh strokes every outline centred on the edge, and dash never compensates:

- **basic:PT-4 (with sil:SD-3, PIC-15).** "twoway strokes bars, histogram bins, areas and rbar inside and honours lalign(); dash centres every stroke and calls lalign() 'the same picture'; rcap caps butt-ended, not round." Measured on bar (P9): the default outline rect "x=836.73 w=394.74" lies inside the fill rect "x 814.05, w 440.10"; under `lalign(outside)` it is "x=791.37 w=485.46"; on area and rarea "the default clips the stroke to the shape and outside switches to an evenodd outside clip". dash_render.py:15668-15677 keeps every `lalign()` word silent ("twoway draws a line the same"), true only for lines (measured on `line mpg weight` alone, extract_varlabel_mlabel_truth.do:116). twoway's rcap caps carry `stroke-linecap=round` (tw100.svg, 74 caps). The help claim dash.sthlp:4782-4786 (Appendix B HR-14) is false for closed shapes.
- **basic:PT-6.** "mlalign(center) and inside draw one picture": twoway gives three SVGs (varlabel_mlabel_truth.json `malign.inside`/`center`/`outside` hashes c035a286/7de52be8/dd63ab73); its default marker is inside ("fill r 21.74, ring r 16.88 w 9.72"). dash draws inside and center alike (dash_render.py:15937-15951) and `_markersize_px` (:12583) returns the nominal diameter with no stroke compensation, so dash's default marker is twoway's *center* picture, half an outline width larger in radius (inferred by the verifier; 47-03-01 measures it).
- **basic:PT-5.** "dlalign(center/outside) changes dot size in twoway; dash drops it silently": default inside "dot outer radius 4.93"; `dlalign(center)` "outer radius 8.17"; outside differs too (dot_truth.json D30 vs D01). The `tw_silent` row came from one `dlalign(inside)` probe (extract_noted_option_truth.do:125) and silences every value on every type; help :6573-6577 (HR-14) and the `_dot_style` docstring (:23704, "named by dash.ado") are stale.

**After this section.** Markers and dots are sized so their outer edge sits where twoway's does for inside (the default), center and outside -- exact, because marker sizes and line widths are both screen pixels. Bars, histogram bins and rbar (vertical and horizontal) draw their outline inset by half its width for inside, outset for outside, centred for center, following resizes and filter moves in both modes. The area's outline ring (34-05), rarea's two bounds and the ci bands' outlines are inset the same way in screen space: every vertex moves half a stroke along the bisector of its two edges, so a sloped edge sits where twoway's does too, and no difference is left to document. rcap caps are round-ended as twoway draws them.

**Why together.** One mechanism -- the stroke-alignment offset -- serves all three items; they share `_apply_line_opts`'s lalign block, `_apply_marker_opts`'s mlalign block and `_dot_style`.

### Section decisions

- **D-47-03.a — The alignment arithmetic (one rule).** For an outline of width `w` px (the glyph's `line_width` as drawn), twoway's alignment `a` maps to a centred Bokeh stroke on a shape grown by `g(a) = {inside: -w/2, center: 0, outside: +w/2}` px on each side. Markers: Bokeh `size` (diameter) = `S + 2*g(a)` = `S - w` (inside), `S` (center), `S + w` (outside), floored at `_DASH_MIN_MARKER_PX = 0.01` so a zero-size marker still draws its stroke (47-06 DG-12 builds on this). Rectangles: each edge moved outward by `g(a)` px. Polygons (34-05's area outline ring, rarea's two bound Lines, each ci band's outline Lines): the stroke path is moved in *screen space* (each axis's scale, linear or log, as for rectangles): each vertex moves `g(a)` px along the bisector of its two edges' outward normals -- the miter offset `g / cos(θ/2)`, θ the angle between the two normals, its length capped at `4*|g|` px -- so a straight edge, level or sloped, moves exactly `g` px at right angles to itself, as twoway's inset stroke lies; the NaN separators between runs are kept; a run whose inset would turn it inside out (its signed screen area changes sign or becomes zero) collapses to its centroid, so it has zero size, as a rectangle's crossing edges meet at their midpoint (D-47-03.c). TR-47-03.5 gives the arithmetic.
- **D-47-03.b — Where the default comes from.** The default alignment is the scheme's `alignstroke` entry for the class (`alignstroke p inside`, and for histogram, plotregion, legend), read through `SchemeParser`'s existing key reader; a scheme without the key is `inside` for markers and closed shapes (s2color's value). `lalign()`, `mlalign()`, `dlalign()` and the style options that carry an align (`lstyle()`, `mlstyle()`, `dlstyle()` resolving to a linestyle with `align`) override it.
- **D-47-03.c — Field sizes and data-unit shapes use transforms, not new columns.** A marker whose `size` is a field (weighted markers, `__dash_wsize*`) gets `size=transform(<field>, CustomJSTransform(args={"g": 2*g(a), "lo": 0.01}, func=..., v_func=...))` -- `max(v + g, lo)` per value -- so the saved page's `dash_rescale_wsizes` and the served refit keep working unchanged (no `_FILTER_JS` edit). A rectangle's outline is a second, outline-only `Quad` over the fill renderer's own source whose four edges are `expr(CustomJSExpr(...))`, each the fill's edge moved `g` px in *screen space* -- on a linear axis by `g * (end - start) / inner size` in data units, on a log axis multiplicatively, `v * exp(g * ln(end / start) / inner size)` -- and a pair of edges that cross (a bar narrower than its outline under inside) meets at its midpoint, so no width or height is ever negative (REVIEW-5 R5-18). The expressions are re-evaluated when the source changes (filter move, both modes) and when the figure's `inner_width`/`inner_height` or ranges change (a `js_on_change` that calls `source.change.emit()`), so the inset is exact in pixels at every size. These are model properties, not `_FILTER_JS`/`_DASH_AXIS_FINISH_JS` text; the section stays a non-JS section (R12).
- **D-47-03.d — The fill keeps its geometry.** twoway's fill rect is exactly `barwidth` wide (PIC-15: "54.34 SVG units = 1.0015 day"); only the outline moves. The fill renderer keeps today's glyph with `line_alpha = 0`; the outline renderer (`fill_alpha = 0`, tags `dash_outline`) carries the stroke, no hover, no legend item, excluded from the brush (null row ids), drawn right after the fill.
- **D-47-03.e — Lines stay as they are.** lalign() on line, connected, spike, dropline, rspike, rcap, rline, pcspike and the fits is silent and unchanged ("On lines (line, rcap, pcspike) twoway is indeed the same").
