## Overview

**What is wrong today.**

- **AX-5.** "custom on xlabel()/ylabel() is ignored: its styles reach every label and the grid; custom with no list is accepted (twoway r(198))" (audit :443). `custom` is in `_DASH_AXLABEL_HONOURED` (dash_render.py:53662-53676) and changes nothing: `ylabel(25 "x", add custom labcolor(red) labsize(large))` draws every y label red and large; `ylabel(25, add custom labcolor(red) grid glcolor(red))` draws the whole grid red where twoway keeps `#F0F0F0`; `ylabel(, custom labcolor(red))` draws, where twoway refuses `tick_styles[-1] invalid name`, r(198) (appendix :1220-1225; `_gs_parse_and_log_tickset.ado:427-438`).
- **AX-6.** "Gridlines of xtick()/xmtick() are drawn in the minor style (dotted); twoway uses the major gridstyle" (audit :446). dash_render.py:44966-44972 sets `minor_grid_line_*` from the scheme look's `minor_grid_*`; `scheme-s2color.scheme:1846` reads `gridstyle minor major`, and every shipped scheme includes s2color (only s2color sets `gridstyle`). The help sentence dash.sthlp:4867 ("grid draws the minor grid in the scheme's minor_grid style") describes the wrong picture (HR-16).
- **AX-8.** "Label sub-options twoway draws are named: labelminlen(), noshowlabels/showlabels, nopgmgrid/pgmgrid, style(); showlabels nolabels hides labels" (audit :452). They are in `_DASH_AXLABEL_SUBOPT_TABLE` (:53574-53604) and missing from the honoured set.
- **AX-14.** "tstyle(), labstyle(), gstyle(), tlstyle(), glstyle() and nogextend are named" (audit :464). `ylabel(, tstyle(major_notick))` removes the ticks in twoway (F12); `nogextend` stops the gridlines at the scale's ends (axis_look_truth.json `gx_nogextend`: 454-3795 against 390-3859). The help names them as differences (dash.sthlp:4566-4572).
- **AX-15 (AX-17, AX-19).** Documented differences to close: `xtick()` and `xmtick()` share one tick length (dash.sthlp:4868-4870); a reference line outside the scale is clipped where twoway draws it in the margin (:5098-5107); `%12s`/`%21x`/`%16H` axis formats are written `%9.0g` with a note (:4741-4751); the ticks of an axis past Stata's dates are natscale over its numbers (:4818-4820); the plot-region margin is fixed at build (:4529-4530, :4639-4643). Dates past the browser's Date range stay the M11 residual (documented, not drawn).

**After this section**, in both modes: `custom` styles only its listed ticks (an overlay), drops grid edits, and is refused without a list as twoway refuses it; tick-option gridlines take the tickset's gridstyle (major on every shipped scheme); `labelminlen()`, `showlabels`/`noshowlabels`, `pgmgrid`/`nopgmgrid` and `style()` are drawn; the five style names are read from Stata's style files; `nogextend` stops the gridlines at the scale; xtick and xmtick draw at their own lengths; out-of-scale reference lines appear in the margin; string, hex and byte-dump formats write twoway's text; past-Stata-date axes take twoway's ticks; the plot-region margin follows a resize. Nothing drawn prints a note.

**Why one section.** All five are the label/tick/grid look of `_dash_parse_axis_spec`/`_dash_apply_axis_spec`/`_dash_apply_tick_option` and the scheme look dict: one owner of `_DASH_AXLABEL_HONOURED` and the tick gridlines (index Owns).

### Section decisions (binding on the executor)

- **D-44-02.a A custom tick is an overlay.** For a label/tick option with `custom`, its listed values are removed from the axis's own tick set (they are not passed to `_dash_axis_add`) and drawn by a composite tagged `dash_custom_ticks`: (1) labels -- a `LabelSet` over a new `ColumnDataSource({"v": values, "t": texts})` with `x="v"` (data, x axis) or `y="v"` (y axis) and the other coordinate in `screen` units at `-(major_tick_out + major_label_standoff)` px from the frame edge on the axis's side (mirrored for `above`/`right`), `text_align`/`text_baseline` as Bokeh draws that side's major labels (`center`/`top` below; `right`/`middle` left), font, size, colour and angle from the axis's look with the custom option's edits applied; (2) ticks -- one `Whisker(base="v", lower=-L, upper=0, lower_units="screen", upper_units="screen", dimension="height"|"width", source=<same>, lower_head=None, upper_head=None)` with `line_color`, `line_width` from the tick look plus the custom edits (`tlength()`/`tposition()` set L and the sign); (3) a gridline at each value in the axis's normal grid style when the axis draws its grid (a second `Grid(dimension=..., ticker=FixedTicker(ticks=values))` tagged `dash_custom_grid`); grid edits (`glcolor` and the other `gl*`, `grid`, `nogrid`) on the custom option are ignored, as `_gs_parse_and_log_tickset.ado:427-438` drops them. ## Risk: [yellow] -- the axis label/tick applier and the axis finisher's margin step run on every axis of every figure

**Why.** `_dash_apply_axis_spec`, `_dash_apply_tick_option` and `dash_axis_finish` touch every axis in both modes; the margin step decides every range.

**Risk factors.**
1. The margin-on-resize change moves every range by up to 1.3% of the plot width after a resize: browser tests that resize (test_default_axes_chrome.py, test_range_probe-based tests) read ranges.
2. The overlay LabelSet/Whisker and the margin Arrow are new annotations the parity guard and `_figure_profile` count; their px must follow `scale()`.
3. New style classes widen the style-file walk (`_style_walk`, memo bounds) and change the pinned class tuple (test_help_claims_9.py:222-230).
4. The format kinds `x` and `H` were refused in other places too (CSV export, hover, colour keys): enabling them on axes must not change those callers unless the fixture says twoway writes them there.
5. JS: `_DASH_AXIS_FINISH_JS` (nogextend, margin), `_DASH_FMT_JS` (the prefix of `_DASH_FILTER_MASK_JS` and so of `_FILTER_JS`, dash_render.py:31654, :31821) and 43-01's dispatcher `_DASH_OVERLAY_JS` (two kinds) are edited. Batch 44.2 holds 44-02 alone (JS = yes in the manifest), so R11/R12 are met.
6. Layout-time work runs on every resize of every figure: two more kinds must not slow the dispatcher or loop.

**Mitigation.**
1. The resize rule equals today's at the built size (a test renders at the built width and asserts identical ranges); the browser tests are run in 44-02-10.
2. Each new model is tagged; `_figure_profile` reads tags; parity entries are added; `ALLOWED` is not edited; `TestCustom::test_scaled` pins the overlay under `scale(1.5)`.
3. test_help_claims_9.py's tuple pin is flipped (44-03 and 45-03 extend it again).
4. `_dash_fmt_refusal` keeps refusing for its non-axis callers through a `where` argument (`where="axis"` enables x/H); callers keep the default.
5. node tests through `test/js_runner.py` pin the JS twins; parity entries move.
6. The two kinds sit behind the dispatcher's signature guard (canvas size, target boxes, and the watched range values of `refline_margin` items, D-44-02.g(2)); 44-02-10 appends one row to `_PAGE_ROWS` of test/test_overlay_budget.py (a figure with an `xline()` outside the scale) and runs 43-01's budget node `DASH_TEST_TIER=release python3 -m pytest test/test_overlay_budget.py::TestOverlayBudget::test_resize_budget -q -n0`, as 43-01 requires of every later dispatcher user.
