## Verdict

The cluster is executable with corrections. The numerical ports are in good shape: every JavaScript estimator twin whose code is in the files (lfit/qfit solver and bands, lowess, lpoly rule of thumb and local fit, mband/mspline, fpfit) reproduces its Python twin on `auto` to 1e-13 under node, the prelude/marker discipline is coherent across S10-S28, and the Stata-side sections (S11, S12, S14, S15, S17, S18, S20, S21, S24, S27) name their files, anchors, refusal texts and flips concretely. Three things must change before execution: the S29 isoband cutter's interior-segment orientation is inverted relative to its boundary edges, so no ring closes (R2-01; the one-line fix is verified); S28's closed-set assertion and S26's `_FROZEN` edit make batch 9's full suite red on landing (R2-05, R2-06); and the tracking ids `B5`/`B4` must become `B5b`/`B4a-c` with the sha-by-amend convention everywhere (R2-09 to R2-11), or `test_tracking_documents.py` never sees these rows close. The remaining majors are plan-coherence gaps between neighbours (S11's frozen points vs S10/S28; S12's residual vs S16's boundaries; S13's same-batch mapper edit; S26's option minimums) that the maintainer can settle with the ratification table above.
